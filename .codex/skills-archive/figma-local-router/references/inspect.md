# Figma Inspection

Inspect the requested Figma source and identify the exact frames/components/states relevant to the task.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the target file/frame/component.
2. Capture layout, sizing, typography, color, assets, variants, and interaction states that affect implementation.
3. Separate design facts from implementation assumptions.
4. Gather only what is needed before moving to extraction or implementation.
