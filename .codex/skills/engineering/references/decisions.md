# Decisions

Use RFC-style documents while a material engineering decision is still open and ADR-style records after a decision is approved. These are documentation modes inside engineering, not separate automatic routes.

## Procedure

1. Describe context, constraints, and the exact decision being considered or recorded.
2. For an RFC, compare viable options and trade-offs without pretending the decision is already made.
3. For an ADR, record the approved choice, rationale, consequences, and superseded decisions if any.
4. Link the decision to the requirement or repository area it governs.

## Guardrails

- Do not create an RFC or ADR for routine implementation choices that do not need durable decision history.
- Do not reopen a settled decision while merely recording it.
